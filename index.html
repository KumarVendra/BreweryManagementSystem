Lemon Jelly Cafe Management Dashboard

Welcome to Lemon Jelly Cafe!

Lemon Jelly Cafe is a cozy local cafe dedicated to serving high-quality coffee, fresh pastries, and delightful snacks. Our goal is to provide a warm and inviting space where customers can relax, work, or catch up with friends while enjoying their favorite beverages.

This management system allows cafe staff to efficiently manage products and orders. You can add new items, view available products, update details, and remove discontinued items — all in a simple and user-friendly interface.
